# Creatures

Creatures are the heart of Inkbound.

## Creature Philosophy

Creatures should be memorable through their silhouette, movement, personality, rarity, and role in the world.

The roster should stay intentionally small during early development so each creature can receive meaningful polish.

## Current Creatures

### Rocko

A foundational creature for the early game.

### Blobster

A simple, expressive creature suited to the collection loop.

### Voidlings

Strange creatures associated with the VoidWastes.

### Mossbit

A Mosswood creature with a glitch-like visual identity.

## Rarity

Creature rarity should affect discovery and collection goals without making common creatures feel useless.

Potential tiers:

- Common
- Uncommon
- Rare
- Epic
- Legendary

Exact tiers and probabilities are TBD.

## Creature Design Checklist

Every new creature should have:

- Distinct silhouette
- Distinct movement
- Clear habitat
- Capture behavior
- Collection value
- Rarity
- Visual identity
