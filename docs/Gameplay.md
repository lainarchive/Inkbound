# Gameplay

## Core Loop

**Explore → Capture → Collect → Farm → Upgrade → Unlock Areas → Find Rarer Creatures**

Inkbound is built around a simple creature-collection loop with short, readable interactions and room for long-term collection progress.

## Capture

Creatures can be captured through a custom interaction rather than Roblox ProximityPrompts.

The capture interaction should feel active without becoming a complicated minigame.

### Capture Goals

- Keep the interaction readable.
- Give the player a reason to pay attention.
- Make rarer creatures feel more meaningful to capture.
- Avoid relying on default Roblox UI.

## Collection

Captured creatures become part of the player's collection and can contribute to progression systems.

## Farming

Collected creatures can be used as part of the player's farming/progression loop, generating income and experience over time.

## Upgrades

Progression should gradually improve the player's ability to explore, capture, collect, and access new content.

## Areas

The world is divided into unlockable areas. Early areas include **Mosswood** and **VoidWastes**.

New areas should introduce new creatures, visual themes, and progression targets rather than simply increasing enemy statistics.
