# World

Inkbound takes place inside a magical storybook-like world where ink is more than decoration: it is part of the world's identity.

## Mosswood

The early-game region. Mosswood should establish Inkbound's cozy, hand-drawn fantasy while introducing the core exploration and creature-collection systems.

## VoidWastes

A stranger region where the visual language becomes unstable. Voidlings and other unusual creatures can be introduced here.

## World Direction

The world should feel:

- Hand-drawn
- Whimsical
- Slightly strange
- Expressive
- Discoverable

Areas should be recognizable through their silhouettes, props, creatures, and atmosphere.

New regions should be documented here before implementation.
